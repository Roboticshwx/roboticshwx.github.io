玻尔兹曼分布的提出

在大多数强化学习的场景中, 对于机器人的最优动作序列的采取都是用的是高斯分布来描述最优动作的分布.

高斯分布描述假设了所有的最优动作都集中在一个均值附近, 但是由于现实物理世界的混沌和复杂性, 往往会陷入到局部最优解当中.

考虑到具身场景下的最优动作分布, 其动作分布必然是非凸的, 对于同一个目标场景, 机器人可以显然采用不同的动作来实现.

第一步: 能量函数的解析计算

按照物理来源, 我们可以将这个能量分为四项, 即满足:

  • : 任务能量, 对于机器人完成特定任务所需的能量消耗, 能量越低表示动作越高效, 越符合任务目标

  • : 运动能量, 机器人自身运动所消耗的能量, 能量越低表示动作越省力

  • : 交互能量, 动作对环境造成的扰动或影响, 能量越低表示动作越安全, 对环境干扰越小

  • : 先验能量, 反映动作的先验偏好或约束, 能量越低表示动作越符合先验知识或约束条件

当机器人的动作使得机器人本体过于接近障碍物的时候, 世界被扰动, 用斥力势场与约束函数描述.

创新点总结

痛点分析

当前的具身智能算法中, 在语义理解上取得了成功, 但是他们在动作生成的过程当中, 是直接进行端到端的动作输出, 对于一个动作空间完全是一个黑盒映射, 其系统内部是不可解释的神经网络权重.

当一个机器人的动作缺乏可解释性和物理边界的时候, 其行为本身就是不可预测, 可能会导致安全隐患和任务失败。

总结其痛点:

解决方法

通过数学方法统一 “动作空间” 的基础, 引入统计力学中的玻尔兹曼能量分布来描述动作空间的概率分布. 机器人的动作将会从黑盒的确定性输出, 转变为基于概率密度函数的采样.

核心优势

  • 可解释性 (Interpretability): 系统内部通过具体的物理能量项来代替不可解释的神经网络权重, 每一个动作的倾向都有确切的物理原因.

  • 安全边界 (Safety Boundaries): 任何试图引发危险的动作, 过度用力, 或者即将碰撞, 其对应的物理能量项会迅速升高, 由于玻尔兹曼分布中的指数压制效应, 这些危险的动作在生成概率上会被抑制到概率为0.

  • 非凸空间下的多模态解 (Multimodal Solutions in Non-Convex Spaces): 玻尔兹曼分布天然支持多峰（Multi-modal）概率密度。当物理能量场存在多个“谷底”（多个等效优解）时，分布会自动在这些谷底形成多个高概率峰值，彻底避免了高斯分布的“模式平均”陷阱，使得机器人能够在复杂的非凸物理空间中保留多重动作假说，并通过采样自然坍缩到最优路径。

核心的创新点如下: 给当下黑盒的机器人动作决策提供了一个安全, 可解释性的框架.

  1. 当前的VLA算法在安全, 省力以及任务约束中基本完全靠数据隐式学习, 没有统一的组合语言. 而我们提出的方法通过引入玻尔兹曼分布, 将动作的能量与其出现概率直接联系起来, 从而为安全、省力和任务约束提供了统一的组合语言.

Literature Review

Introduction

Despite the remarkable zero-shot generalization capabilities demonstrated by recent Vision-Language-Action (VLA) architectures and World Models, their low-level action generation mechanisms suffer from fundamental limitations. Currently, most embodied policies operate as black-box mappings that output either deterministic commands or probability densities modeled by unimodal Gaussian distributions.

This paradigm introduces three critical vulnerabilities when deployed in dynamically complex and contact-rich physical environments:
(1) a lack of physical interpretability;
(2) the absence of explicit safety boundaries;
(3) the inability to capture multiple viable solutions in non-convex physical spaces, often leading to catastrophic mode-averaging behaviors

(e.g., averaging a “go-left” and “go-right” obstacle avoidance strategy into a “crash-straight” action).

To overcome these intrinsic limitations, we propose a paradigm shift from black-box regression to a physics-informed probabilistic framework. Operating within a unified action space, we formulate the action generation process as sampling from a probability density function governed by a Boltzmann energy distribution:

where models the comprehensive physical energy landscape of the state-action pair, and represents the temperature parameter. This energy-based formulation endows the embodied intelligence system with three profound architectural advantages:

  1. Explicit Physical Interpretability (The White-Box Advantage):
    Unlike uninterpretable neural network weights, the total energy landscape is transparently decoupled into meaningful physical components, such as task-driven potential energy, kinematic damping, and interaction strain energy. Every generated action and its evolutionary gradient (via Langevin dynamics) are mathematically traceable to distinct physical forces, transforming the “black-box policy” into a transparent physical process.

  2. Mathematically Guaranteed Safety Boundaries:
    According to statistical mechanics, any action hypothesis that violates physical constraints—such as exerting excessive contact forces or imminent collisions—results in an instantaneous surge in the energy function . Driven by the exponential decay property of the Boltzmann distribution, the probability density of such hazardous actions abruptly collapses to near-zero. This intrinsically establishes a rigorous safety boundary, decoupling high-level semantic hallucination from low-level physical compliance.

  3. Multi-modal Action Generation in Non-convex Spaces:
    Real-world physical tasks are inherently non-convex, frequently admitting multiple optimal strategies. While traditional Gaussian distributions force a single mean, inevitably leading to mode-averaging failures, the Boltzmann distribution naturally accommodates multi-modal probability densities. If the physical energy landscape contains multiple local minima (e.g., grasping an object from distinct viable angles), the Boltzmann distribution seamlessly maintains multiple probability peaks. By integrating this with Langevin dynamics sampling, the agent retains diverse action hypotheses in non-convex spaces and dynamically collapses into a valid, safe mode, strictly avoiding infeasible interpolated actions.
